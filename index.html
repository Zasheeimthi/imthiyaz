04 / APPROACH

Less guesswork. More signal.

I work at the intersection of product thinking, visual clarity, and implementation reality. That means asking the right questions early, making the invisible visible, and staying close to the people who will use the thing.

01

Frame

Understand the audience, the business tension, and what “better” needs to mean before drawing a screen.

02

Find the flow

Map information architecture, user journeys, and the moments where a product should feel most effortless.

03

Make it tangible

Move from low-fidelity wireframes to high-fidelity prototypes that make conversations specific and useful.

04

Test the story

Use usability testing and feedback to find friction, then iterate until the interface earns its clarity.

05

Bridge to build

Bring a front-end perspective into the handoff so the intended experience survives contact with the browser.

07 / POINT OF VIEW

Use AI to move faster. Stay human.

AI is useful when it helps a team see more possibilities, not when it pretends to know the user. I use it as a thinking partner for research synthesis, early exploration, content variations, and explaining a decision from multiple angles.

The judgment stays human: what matters, what is inclusive, what is actually useful, and what still needs to be tested with real people.

01Compress the noise

Turn large inputs into patterns worth discussing.

02Expand the options

Explore directions before the team commits.

03Return to reality

Validate the idea with people, context, and evidence.
